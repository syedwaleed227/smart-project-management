# 18. Pages and Screens

Grouped by area. Each screen respects role + department scope.

## Auth & shell
- **Login** (with 2FA), Forgot/Reset password, SSO option.
- **Global shell** — top bar (search, notifications, profile), left nav, breadcrumb.
- **Notification center**, **Profile & preferences**, **Switch role/department** (multi-role users).

## Dashboards
- **Main dashboard** (role-aware landing).
- **CEO/Owner**, **Project Manager**, **Finance**, **Department Head**, **Employee**, **Client**, **Admin** dashboards. (See [Dashboards](12-dashboards-and-reporting.md).)

## Projects & tasks
- **Projects list** + filters; **Project detail** (overview, tasks, milestones, budget, expenses, risks, issues, documents, meetings, activity).
- **Project dashboard** (health, progress, budget vs actual).
- **Task board** — Kanban / List / Calendar views; **My tasks**, **Team tasks**, **Overdue**, **Workload view**.
- **Task detail** — comments, attachments, time logs, dependencies, approval.

## Finance
- **Finance dashboard**; **Budgets**; **Expenses**; **Purchase requests / POs**; **Invoices** (client & vendor); **Payments**; **Reimbursements / Advances / Petty cash**; **P&L**, **Cash flow**, **Profitability** reports.

## Meetings
- **Meeting calendar**; **Meeting detail** (agenda, attendees, minutes, action items); **Create/edit meeting**.

## Approvals
- **Approval center** — unified pending queue; **Approval detail** (context, history, approve/reject with comment); **Workflow builder** (admin).

## People & relationships
- **HR / Employees** list + profile (attendance, leave, documents, performance, assignments).
- **Leave & attendance** screens.
- **Clients** list + profile; **Vendors** list + profile.
- **Client portal** (external) — their projects, updates, invoices, meetings, docs.
- **Vendor portal** (external) — POs, submit invoices, payment status, docs.

## Reports, docs, admin
- **Reports** hub (all reports, filters, export, scheduled, AI report).
- **Documents** library (categories, versions, permissions, search).
- **User management**; **Role management** (RBAC); **Department management**.
- **Settings** (company, finance/tax, approval thresholds, notification policy, integrations, security/2FA, backups).
- **Audit log viewer** (Admin/Auditor).
